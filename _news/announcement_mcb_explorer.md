Our [MCB Explorer](https://jianhao1991.github.io/mcb-explorer/) is now live! This interactive web app lets you explore the efficacy of different marine cloud brightening (MCB) seeding strategies by adjusting seeding rate, duration, and start time. It runs Gaussian-process emulators trained on our large-eddy simulation ensembles of stratocumulus-to-cumulus transitions in the northeast Pacific, entirely in your browser. Have fun playing it! :computer: Papers forthcoming! :page_with_curl:
